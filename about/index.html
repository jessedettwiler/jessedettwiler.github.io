About

Swiss composer · Folk-Hop creator · Organic beats

I’m C4C — a Swiss composer and producer known for creating Folk-Hop: a blend of acoustic indie folk instrumentation with lo-fi hip-hop beats.

I started releasing music independently in 2020 with Folk-Hop Vol. 1 (including a vinyl edition). Since then, I’ve connected with hundreds of amazing artists, worked with 20+ record labels, and reached 300M+ streams across platforms.

In spring 2025, I founded Folk-Hop Radio: a playlist network built to support new Folk-Hop artists and give the sub-genre the visibility it still rarely gets.

If you’re working on any creative project (video games, films, OSTs, etc.) where you feel this warm acoustic-lo-fi world would fit, don’t hesitate to reach out.

What is Folk-Hop?

Folk-Hop is a sub-style of lo-fi that blends the warmth of indie folk (acoustic guitars, ukulele, organic textures) with the groove and bounce of hip-hop-inspired beats.

Think: lo-fi beats with a folk soul.

Core ingredients

  • Acoustic instruments (guitar, ukulele, mandolin)
  • Nature-inspired textures and ambient layers
  • Lo-fi drums with hip-hop swing
  • Warm, human, organic production

Where it fits

  • Study / focus
  • Relaxation / calm routines
  • Creative work (writing, design, games)
  • Film / documentary / indie visuals

FAQ

Is Folk-Hop a genre or a playlist concept?

It started as a genre idea, but it also became a playlist culture. It’s both: a sound and a world.

Do you use AI to create music?

No. My work is fully human-made. Folk-Hop is rooted in organic textures, real instruments, and human imperfections.

Can I license your music for a project?

Yes — film, games, YouTube projects, podcasts, and more. Contact me and tell me what you’re working on.
